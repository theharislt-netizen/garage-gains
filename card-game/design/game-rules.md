# Game Rules

*(Working title — a house variant in the Shithead / Palace / Karma family of shedding card games)*

## Overview

This is a shedding card game: the goal is to be the **first player to get rid of every card** you hold — your hand, then your face-up cards, then your face-down cards. The last player left holding cards loses.

A standard 52-card deck is used. There are **three special cards — 2, 5, and 10** — that break the normal rules, described in detail below.

---

## 1. Setup

1. Shuffle a standard 52-card deck.
2. Deal each player:
   - **3 face-down cards**, placed in front of them, unseen (the "blind" cards).
   - **3 face-up cards**, dealt on top of the face-down cards, visible to everyone.
   - A **starting hand of 2 cards**, dealt to each player. After each play you draw back up to 2 (until the draw pile is empty).
3. Place the remaining cards in the middle of the table, face-down, as the **draw pile**.
4. Leave space next to the draw pile for the **discard pile**, which starts empty.

---

## 2. Basic Turn Structure

On your turn, you must either:

- **Play** one card, or a matching set of 2, 3, or 4 cards of the *same rank*, from your hand onto the discard pile, **or**
- If you cannot legally play, **pick up the entire discard pile** into your hand.

### Rules for playing a card

- A card played onto the discard pile must be **equal to or higher in rank** than the top card currently on the pile.
- You may play multiple cards of the same rank together in one turn (e.g., two 6s, three 9s, all four Kings), as long as that rank is equal to or higher than the top card.
- **Completing a four-of-a-kind** on the discard pile (the fourth card of the same rank landing on top) automatically **burns the pile** — same effect as playing a 10 (see below).
- After playing, **draw back up to 2 cards** from the draw pile (once the draw pile is empty, you stop drawing and your hand will start shrinking below 2).
- If you cannot play a legal card, you must **pick up the whole discard pile** and add it to your hand.

---

## 3. The Three Special Cards

Special cards can be played **on top of any card, regardless of rank**, and **at any time it's your turn** — you do not need to be "stuck" or unable to beat the top card to play one. This is what makes them special: they ignore the equal-or-higher rule entirely.

### 🃏 2 — Wildcard / Reset

- Can be played on top of *any* card, at *any* time on your turn.
- Playing a 2 **resets the pile**: the rank requirement is cleared, so the **next player can play any card they like**, regardless of what came before.
- It does not burn or remove the pile from the game — it stays in place, just with its "value" reset.

### 🃏 5 — Wildcard + Bonus Play

- Can be played on top of *any* card, at *any* time on your turn.
- Playing a 5 also **resets the pile**, exactly like a 2.
- **Bonus play:** immediately after playing the 5, you get to play **one additional card from your hand**, of **any rank you choose**.
- **Hand-size floor:** if playing the 5 (and/or the bonus card) would drop your hand below **2 cards**, you draw back up from the draw pile **first**, so you always have at least 2 cards in hand to choose your bonus play from.

### 🃏 10 — Burn

- Can be played on top of *any* card, at *any* time on your turn.
- Playing a 10 **burns the entire discard pile** — all cards on the pile are removed from the game completely (they do not go into anyone's hand and are not reused).
- The burner’s **turn then ends**. Play passes to the next player, who faces an empty pile and may play any card. Completing a four-of-a-kind burns the same way — no extra move.

---

## 4. Running Out of Hand Cards

- The instant both your hand **and** the draw pile are empty, all **3 face-up cards** move into your hand as a batch (all 3 at once — not one at a time, and not played directly off the table). From that point you play those cards from your hand as normal.
- This pickup happens **before** any 5 bonus play or draw-up-to-2 check. If you empty your hand-minus-draw-pile by playing a 5 at this exact boundary, the sequence is: scoop the 3 face-up cards into hand, **then** draw up to 2 if needed and pick the bonus card from that new hand.
- Once those cards (and any later hand cards) are gone, you move on to your **3 face-down (blind) cards**:
  - Flip one over blind — you don't know what it is until it's revealed.
  - If it beats the top of the pile, it's played.
  - If it doesn't, you pick up the entire discard pile (including the blind card you just flipped) into your hand, and go back to playing from your hand again.

---

## 5. Winning and Losing

- The **first player to get rid of all their cards** — hand, then face-up, then face-down — wins (and is out of the game).
- Play continues among the remaining players until only one player is left holding cards.
- That final remaining player is the **loser**.

---

## 6. Quick Reference

| Card | Effect | When playable |
|---|---|---|
| **2** | Resets the pile — next player can play anything | Anytime, on any card |
| **5** | Resets the pile + play 1 bonus card from hand (draw up to 2 cards first if needed) | Anytime, on any card |
| **10** | Burns the pile completely out of the game; turn then passes | Anytime, on any card |
| Any other card | Must be equal or higher rank than the top card | Only if it beats the top card |
| 4-of-a-kind completed on pile | Burns the pile (same as a 10) | — |
